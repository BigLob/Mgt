# Archenemy: Official Rules Summary

Based on Comprehensive Rules section 904 (and 701.32, 701.33, 314) plus Wizards' format pages.
Rule text is paraphrased, not verbatim. Check the current Comprehensive Rules for exact wording.

## Standard Archenemy (CR 904.1-904.11)
- **Setup (904.1-904.2):** A team of any size faces one player, the archenemy. It's a two-team game with the "attack multiple players" and "shared team turns" options. Wizards recommends 4+ players (3+ heroes).
- **Decks:** Everyone brings a normal 60+ card deck. The archenemy also has a scheme deck of at least 20 cards, with no more than two cards of the same English name (904.3).
- **Zone (904.4):** Scheme cards stay in the command zone all game, whether in the deck or face up.
- **Life (904.5):** The archenemy starts at 40. Every other player starts at 20.
- **First turn (904.6):** The archenemy goes first. Wizards says they still draw on their first turn.
- **Ownership (904.7):** The player who started with a scheme in their command zone owns it. A face-up scheme's controller is its owner.
- **Abilities (904.8):** A face-up scheme's abilities work from the command zone.
- **Setting in motion (904.9, 701.32):** Right after the archenemy's precombat main phase begins, they turn the top scheme face up. This does not use the stack, and "when you set this scheme in motion" abilities trigger. Only the archenemy can do this, one scheme at a time.
- **Non-ongoing schemes (904.10):** At the next time a player would get priority, once its triggers are off the stack, it goes face down to the bottom of the scheme deck. This is a state-based action.
- **Ongoing schemes (904.11, 701.33):** They stay face up until an ability tells you to abandon them. Abandoning turns it face down and puts it on the bottom of the scheme deck.

## Heroes' turns and winning (Wizards format page)
- The team takes its turn together: beginning phase, main phases, and attacks are declared at the same time.
- The archenemy assigns each attacker to a specific player or planeswalker.
- The team declares blockers together and can block attacks aimed at teammates.
- When a hero reaches 0 life and loses, all cards they own are removed from the game.
- The game ends when the archenemy loses or all heroes have lost. If the archenemy loses, all opponents win, including those already eliminated (MTG Wiki notes).

## Supervillain Rumble (CR 904.12)
- A free-for-all where every player has their own scheme deck and is an archenemy.
- Only the "attack multiple players" option is used.
- The starting player is random.
- Wizards says each player starts at 40 life.

## Archenemy Commander (CR 904.13, added with Duskmourn Commander)
- Normal Commander rules apply, with Archenemy modifications.
- Each player brings a 100-card Commander deck.
- The archenemy starts at 60 life. The hero team shares one 60-life total under Two-Headed Giant rules (810.8, 810.9).
- Poison is not shared. The archenemy loses at 10 poison counters, and so does any single hero at 10 (904.13c).
- The scheme deck needs at least 10 cards, each with a different English name (904.13d).
- Heroes take shared turns but do not share cards, mana, or other resources. They can block for each other.
- Some older schemes, such as Mortal Flesh Is Weak, don't work properly with a shared life total, and Wizards recommends skipping them.
